About Me

I'm Abdulvaliy Saydalixonov, a developer and founder from Uzbekistan interested in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. I'm passionate about building technology that solves real-world problems.

My main academic interests are AI, Machine Learning, and software engineering, with cybersecurity as a secondary area of interest.

I enjoy taking ideas from concept to production. I'm the founder of SA-IELTS , an independent platform built to make authentic IELTS practice more accessible. What started as an idea became a product serving users across more than 30 countries.

I also serve as Chief Technology Officer at Portail , where I contribute to software architecture, production development, technical decisions, team coordination, and deployment. Working on a real product has taught me a lot about engineering beyond simply writing code.

Outside of product development, I explore AI, experiment with new technologies, and continuously improve my understanding of computer science. I'm particularly interested in automation, problem-solving, and finding elegant solutions to complicated technical challenges.

I believe the best way to understand technology is to build with it. Rather than simply learning concepts, I try to turn what I learn into projects, experiments, and products that people can actually use.

This website is where I share some of that journey: things I'm building, technologies I'm exploring, lessons I've learned, and ideas worth thinking about.

In short, I'm Abdulvaliy — a developer, founder, and lifelong learner interested in building meaningful technology.
